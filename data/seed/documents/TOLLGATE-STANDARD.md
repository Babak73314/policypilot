# Tollgate Auto Insurance — Tollgate Standard Driver — Policy Wording

Version 2026.1 · Fictional document for a software demo. Not a real insurance contract.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this wording, "we", "us" and "our" mean Tollgate Auto Insurance. "You" and "your" mean the policyholder named in the Policy Schedule. "Vehicle" means the car described in the Policy Schedule. "Driver" means you and any person named in the Policy Schedule as a driver.

1.2 "Accident" means a sudden, unexpected event that causes injury or damage. "Liability Limit" means the most we will pay for all claims arising from one Accident. "Policy Year" means each period of 12 months starting on the start date or a renewal date.

1.3 "Collision Deductible" means the amount of each covered collision claim that you pay. We pay the rest of the repair cost or, if the Vehicle is a total loss, its market value at the time of loss less the Collision Deductible.

## 2. Cover

2.1 Liability. We will pay damages you are legally liable to pay for injury to other people or damage to their property caused by an Accident involving the Vehicle, up to the Liability Limit of $100,000 for each Accident. We will also pay legal costs that we agree in advance.

2.2 Collision. We will pay for damage to the Vehicle caused by a collision with another vehicle or object, or by rollover, less the Collision Deductible of $1,000.

2.3 Theft and weather damage. This policy does not cover theft of the Vehicle or damage from fire, storm, flood or hail. Windscreen and glass damage is covered only if it happens in a collision, and then it is subject to the Collision Deductible.

2.4 Roadside assistance. We will send help 24 hours a day if the Vehicle breaks down or cannot be driven. Each Policy Year you may make up to 2 call-outs, and we will tow the Vehicle up to 25 miles to the nearest repairer.

2.5 Rental car. This policy does not include a rental car while the Vehicle is being repaired.

## 3. Eligibility

3.1 Each Driver must be aged between 18 and 85 and must hold a full, valid licence. You cannot be accepted for this policy if you have been refused renewal by another insurer for fraud.

3.2 You must not have made more than 4 claims in the 5 years before the start date. We count every claim, whether or not you were at fault, but we do not count claims that were fully recovered from another insurer.

3.3 Young drivers. Drivers aged 18 to 24 are accepted, but each must be named in the Policy Schedule, and we apply an age-based surcharge to the annual payment that is set when we quote and is not capped. Anyone not named in the Policy Schedule who is aged under 25 is not covered.

3.4 The Vehicle must be a car for private use that is registered and garaged in one of our regions. You must tell us before you change the Vehicle, the garaging address or the main driver.

## 4. Exclusions

4.1 We will not pay for any claim that arises while the Vehicle is being used in racing, rallies, speed trials or on a track, or when the Driver is driving without a valid licence.

4.2 We will not pay for any claim that arises while the Driver is over the legal alcohol limit, is under the influence of drugs, or refuses to take a test.

4.3 We will not pay for theft of the Vehicle or its contents, or for damage from fire, flood, storm or hail, because this policy is not comprehensive.

4.4 We will not pay for wear and tear, mechanical breakdown or tyre damage unless it is caused by a collision.

## 5. Waiting periods

5.1 Cover starts at the date and time shown in the Policy Schedule. There is no waiting period for liability cover or collision cover.

5.2 A new Driver added during the Policy Year is covered from the time we confirm the change in writing. A change of Vehicle is covered from the time we confirm the change.

## 6. Claims

6.1 Report every Accident to us within 48 hours, and every theft or vandalism to the police within 24 hours. Do not admit liability or agree to a settlement without our consent.

6.2 We will decide the claim within 20 working days of receiving everything we asked for. If we cannot decide in that time we will tell you why and give you a new date.

## 7. Cancellation

7.1 You have a cooling-off period of 14 days from the start date or the date you receive your documents, whichever is later. If you cancel within this period and have made no claim, we will refund the full payment.

7.2 After the cooling-off period you may cancel the annual policy at any time by writing to us. We will refund the unused part of your annual payment pro rata, which means in proportion to the number of days left in the Policy Year. We will deduct a cancellation fee of $40 from the refund.

7.3 We may cancel the policy by giving you 14 days' written notice. We may cancel immediately for fraud, for non-payment after the notice in section 5, or if the Vehicle is sold. If we cancel, we will refund the unused part of the annual payment pro rata, with no fee.

## 8. Complaints

8.1 If you are unhappy with our service or a decision, please write to complaints@tollgate-auto.example.com. Include your policy number and a description of the problem. We will confirm that we received your complaint within 3 working days and send a final response within 30 days.

8.2 If you are not satisfied with our final response, or we have not responded within 30 days, you may refer the matter to the Fictional Insurance Ombudsman Service, a free and independent dispute service created for this demonstration. You must refer the complaint within 6 months of the date of our final response. The Ombudsman Service can make a decision that binds us but not you, and using it does not affect your right to take legal action.
