# Solace Health Cooperative — Solace Silver Choice — Policy Wording

Version 2026.1 · Fictional document for a software demo. Not a real insurance contract.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this wording, "we", "us" and "our" mean Solace Health Cooperative. "You" and "your" mean the member named in the Policy Schedule. "Dependent" means your spouse or partner, and your children under age 26, who are listed in the Policy Schedule.

1.2 "Deductible" means the amount of covered charges you pay each policy year before we start to share the cost. "Coinsurance" means the percentage of covered charges you pay after the Deductible has been met. "Out-of-Pocket Maximum" means the most you pay in a policy year in Deductible and Coinsurance for covered services.

1.3 "Network" means the hospitals, doctors and other providers that have an agreement with us. "Pre-authorisation" means our written approval for a service before you receive it. "Planned Surgery" means surgery that is not an emergency and can be scheduled in advance.

## 2. Cover

2.1 Your Deductible is $3,000 per member per policy year. After you have paid it, you pay 30 percent of covered charges (Coinsurance) and we pay the rest, until you reach the Out-of-Pocket Maximum of $7,000 per member per policy year. After that we pay 100 percent of covered charges for the rest of the policy year.

2.2 Network. This plan uses a broad Network of hospitals and specialists. Non-emergency care outside the Network is covered at a Coinsurance that is 20 percentage points higher than the in-network rate, and counts toward your Out-of-Pocket Maximum.

2.3 Telehealth. Video and phone consultations with Network doctors are covered and the Deductible does not apply to them.

2.4 Preventive care. Annual check-ups, recommended screenings and routine vaccinations are covered in full in the Network and are not subject to the Deductible.

2.5 Dental add-on. You may choose the optional dental add-on when you apply or at renewal. It covers check-ups, fillings and extractions, and major dental work such as crowns, up to the annual dental limit in your add-on schedule. Without the add-on, dental care is not covered.

## 3. Eligibility

3.1 You must be aged between 18 and 64 at the start date to be accepted as the main member. Cover for you may continue after age 64 for as long as the policy is renewed.

3.2 You and your Dependents must live in the north, central or south region. If you move outside these regions you must tell us within 30 days, and cover ends at the next renewal.

3.3 You may add up to 6 Dependents to your policy. Dependent children are covered until the end of the month in which they turn 26.

## 4. Exclusions

4.1 Cosmetic treatment. We do not cover treatment whose main purpose is to change appearance, unless it is reconstructive surgery after an accident, illness or cancer treatment.

4.2 Experimental treatment. We do not cover treatment, drugs or devices that are not approved for general use, or that are used in clinical trials, unless we agree in writing before treatment.

4.3 Planned Surgery during the waiting period. We do not cover Planned Surgery begun within 60 days of the start date (see section 5).

4.4 We do not cover injuries from professional sport, treatment from providers who are not licensed, services that are free to you under a government programme, or care that is not medically necessary.

## 5. Waiting periods

5.1 Planned Surgery. We do not pay for Planned Surgery that begins within 60 days of the start date. Emergency surgery is always covered from day one.

5.2 Maternity. Maternity care, including delivery, is covered only for pregnancies that begin after you have been covered for 10 months. A baby born to a member who has met this period is covered from birth if you tell us within 30 days.

5.3 Dental add-on. If you choose the dental add-on, major dental work is covered only after the add-on has been in force for 6 months. Check-ups and fillings are covered after 30 days.

5.4 Emergency care, accident treatment, primary care and prescription drugs have no waiting period.

## 6. Claims

6.1 Pre-authorisation. You must obtain Pre-authorisation for planned hospital admissions and for MRI, CT and PET scans. Ask at least 3 working days ahead. If you do not, we will reduce the benefit by 25 percent. Specialist visits do not need approval, and cost a fixed copay of $50, which counts toward your Out-of-Pocket Maximum.

6.2 Emergency care outside the Network. Emergency care is covered at any hospital at the in-network Deductible and Coinsurance. We will pay the reasonable charge, and you are not responsible for the balance. Tell us within 72 hours of an emergency admission.

6.3 We will pay or decline a claim within 30 days of receiving the documents we need. We will give you an explanation of benefits for each claim, showing the amount applied to your Deductible and Out-of-Pocket Maximum.

## 7. Cancellation

7.1 You have a cooling-off period of 14 days starting on the date you receive your policy documents. If you cancel within this period we will refund all payments, less the cost of any claims we have paid.

7.2 After the cooling-off period you may cancel at any time by giving us 30 days' written notice. Payments are monthly, so cancellation takes effect at the end of the month in which the notice period ends, and no refund is given for a month already started.

7.3 We may cancel the policy if payments are more than 30 days late, if you give false information, or if you move outside our regions. We will give you at least 30 days' written notice.

## 8. Complaints

8.1 If you are unhappy with our service or a decision, please write to complaints@solace-health.example.com. Include your member number and a description of the problem. We will confirm that we received your complaint within 3 working days and send a final response within 30 days.

8.2 If you are not satisfied with our final response, or we have not responded within 30 days, you may refer the matter to the Fictional Insurance Ombudsman Service, a free and independent dispute service created for this demonstration. You must refer the complaint within 6 months of the date of our final response. The Ombudsman Service can make a decision that binds us but not you, and using it does not affect your right to take legal action.
