# Kestrel Life Assurance — Kestrel Term Life 20 — Policy Wording

Version 2026.1 · Fictional document for a software demo. Not a real insurance contract.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this wording, "we", "us" and "our" mean Kestrel Life Assurance. "You" and "your" mean the person named as the life insured in the Policy Schedule, who is also the policyholder unless the Policy Schedule says otherwise.

1.2 "Sum Insured" means the amount of life cover shown in your Policy Schedule. "Term" means the period of 20 years starting on the Issue Date. "Issue Date" means the date we accept your application and the first payment is received.

1.3 "Beneficiary" means the person or persons you have named to receive the death benefit.

1.4 "Terminal Illness" means an illness that a registered medical practitioner approved by us certifies is expected to cause death within 12 months, with no reasonable prospect of cure.

## 2. Cover

2.1 We will pay the Sum Insured to your Beneficiary if you die during the Term, provided that all payments due have been made. The Sum Insured available under this policy is from $50,000 to $2,000,000. The Term is 20 years and the benefit is level, which means it does not change during the Term except as described in this section.

2.2 Accelerated benefit. If you are diagnosed with a Terminal Illness during the Term, you may ask us to advance up to 50 percent of the Sum Insured. We will pay the advance to you, and we will reduce the death benefit paid later by the amount advanced. Only one advance is payable under this policy.

2.3 Conversion option. Before you reach age 65 you may convert all or part of the Sum Insured to a permanent whole-of-life policy that we offer at the time, without any new medical questions. The conversion must be requested in writing at least 30 days before your 65th birthday, and the new policy is rated on your age at conversion.

2.4 Grace period. If a payment is missed we will keep your cover in force for 31 days after the due date. If you die during the grace period, we will pay the Sum Insured less the overdue payment. If the payment is not made by the end of the grace period, the policy lapses.

## 3. Eligibility

3.1 You must be aged between 18 and 60 at your last birthday on the Issue Date to apply for this policy. The Sum Insured must be within the range stated in clause 2.1.

3.2 You must live in the north, central or south region, which are the only regions in which we offer this policy, and you must remain a resident of one of them when you apply. You must answer all health, occupation and lifestyle questions fully and honestly.

## 4. Exclusions

4.1 Suicide. We will not pay the Sum Insured if your death is caused by suicide, whether sane or insane, within 2 years of the Issue Date. In that case we will refund the payments you have made, less any amount already advanced.

4.2 Criminal acts. We will not pay the Sum Insured if your death results from you committing or attempting to commit a criminal offence that is punishable by imprisonment.

4.3 Misrepresentation. We will not pay a claim, and may cancel the policy, if you gave us false or incomplete information on your application that would have led us to decline the policy or to offer it on different terms.

4.4 There is no general exclusion for hazardous pursuits or dangerous sports under this policy. Death caused by these activities is covered unless one of the exclusions in this section applies.

## 5. Waiting periods

5.1 Cover begins on the Issue Date. For death from natural causes there is no separate waiting period, apart from the 2-year suicide period set out in clause 4.1.

5.2 The accelerated benefit in clause 2.2 is not available for the first 90 days after the Issue Date, unless the Terminal Illness is the result of an accident.

5.3 There is no waiting period before you can exercise the conversion option. You may convert at any time after the Issue Date and before the conversion deadline.

5.4 Contestability period. For 2 years after the Issue Date, we may review the application and decline a claim if the application contained a material misstatement. After that period we will contest a claim only for fraud.

## 6. Claims

6.1 Your Beneficiary or personal representative must tell us about a death as soon as reasonably possible and, in any case, within 12 months. Late notice will not reduce the claim if it was not reasonably possible to notify us earlier.

6.2 To claim the accelerated benefit, you must send us a medical report written by the treating specialist. We may arrange an independent examination at our cost.

6.3 We will decide the claim within 30 days of receiving all the documents we asked for and will pay within 10 working days after the decision. If we decline a claim we will explain why in writing and describe how to ask for a review.

## 7. Cancellation

7.1 You have a cooling-off period of 14 days starting on the Issue Date or the date you receive your documents, whichever is later. If you cancel within this period we will refund every payment you made, provided that no claim has been made.

7.2 After the cooling-off period you may cancel at any time by writing to us. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period for which you have paid. Term life cover has no cash value, so no refund is given for a period that has already started.

## 8. Complaints

8.1 If you are unhappy with our service or a decision, please write to complaints@kestrel-life.example.com. Include your policy number and a description of the problem. We will confirm that we received your complaint within 3 working days and send a final response within 30 days.

8.2 If you are not satisfied with our final response, or we have not responded within 30 days, you may refer the matter to the Fictional Insurance Ombudsman Service, a free and independent dispute service created for this demonstration. You must refer the complaint within 6 months of the date of our final response. The Ombudsman Service can make a decision that binds us but not you, and using it does not affect your right to take legal action.
