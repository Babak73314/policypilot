# Evergreen Mutual — Evergreen Term Life 20 Flex — Policy Wording

Version 2026.1 · Fictional document for a software demo. Not a real insurance contract.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this wording, "we", "us" and "our" mean Evergreen Mutual. "You" and "your" mean the person named as the life insured in the Policy Schedule, who is also the policyholder unless the Policy Schedule says otherwise.

1.2 "Sum Insured" means the amount of life cover shown in your Policy Schedule. "Term" means the period of 20 years starting on the Issue Date. "Issue Date" means the date we accept your application and the first payment is received.

1.3 "Beneficiary" means the person or persons you have named to receive the death benefit.

1.4 "Hazardous Pursuit" means an activity listed in our hazardous pursuits guide at the time of your application, such as motor racing, rock climbing, scuba diving below 30 metres, powered aviation as a pilot, or parachuting.

## 2. Cover

2.1 We will pay the Sum Insured to your Beneficiary if you die during the Term, provided that all payments due have been made. The Sum Insured available under this policy is from $50,000 to $1,500,000. The Term is 20 years and the benefit is level, which means it does not change during the Term except as described in this section.

2.2 No accelerated benefit. This policy does not include any advance of the Sum Insured on diagnosis of a Terminal Illness. The Sum Insured is payable only on death.

2.3 Conversion option. You may convert all or part of the Sum Insured to a permanent policy that we offer at the time, without new medical questions, until the end of the 15th policy year or your 60th birthday, whichever comes first. The request must be made in writing at least 60 days before that date.

2.4 Reduce cover. Once in each policy year, you may ask us in writing to reduce the Sum Insured to any amount that is not less than $50,000. We do not require a new application, and the reduction takes effect from the next payment date. A reduction cannot be reversed.

2.5 Dividends. This policy is eligible to share in the members' annual dividend that Evergreen Mutual may declare. Any dividend is fictional for this demonstration, is not guaranteed, and does not form part of the Sum Insured.

2.6 Grace period. If a payment is missed we will keep your cover in force for 30 days after the due date. If you die during the grace period, we will pay the Sum Insured less the overdue payment. If the payment is not made by the end of the grace period, the policy lapses.

## 3. Eligibility

3.1 You must be aged between 18 and 60 at your last birthday on the Issue Date to apply for this policy. The Sum Insured must be within the range stated in clause 2.1.

3.2 You must live in the north, central or south region, which are the only regions in which we offer this policy, and you must remain a resident of one of them when you apply. You must answer all health, occupation and lifestyle questions fully and honestly.

3.3 You must tell us about any Hazardous Pursuit you take part in when you apply, and again within 60 days if you take one up during the Term.

## 4. Exclusions

4.1 Suicide. We will not pay the Sum Insured if your death is caused by suicide, whether sane or insane, within 2 years of the Issue Date. In that case we will refund the payments you have made, less any amount already advanced.

4.2 Hazardous Pursuits. We may exclude death arising from a Hazardous Pursuit that you declared at application. Any such exclusion will be written on your Policy Schedule and applies only to the activity named. We will not pay for death arising from a Hazardous Pursuit that you did not declare.

## 5. Waiting periods

5.1 Cover begins on the Issue Date. For death from natural causes there is no separate waiting period, apart from the 2-year suicide period set out in clause 4.1.

5.2 There is no waiting period for the death benefit because this policy contains no accelerated benefit.

5.3 You may not exercise the conversion option during the first policy year. After the first policy anniversary, you may convert at any time before the conversion deadline.

5.4 The right to reduce cover under clause 2.4 is available only after the first policy anniversary.

5.5 Contestability period. For 1 year after the Issue Date, we may review the application and decline a claim if the application contained a material misstatement. After that period we will contest a claim only for fraud.

## 6. Claims

6.1 Your Beneficiary or personal representative must tell us about a death as soon as reasonably possible and, in any case, within 12 months. Late notice will not reduce the claim if it was not reasonably possible to notify us earlier.

6.2 We will decide the claim within 30 days of receiving all the documents we asked for and will pay within 10 working days after the decision. If we decline a claim we will explain why in writing and describe how to ask for a review.

## 7. Cancellation

7.1 You have a cooling-off period of 14 days starting on the Issue Date or the date you receive your documents, whichever is later. If you cancel within this period we will refund every payment you made, provided that no claim has been made.

7.2 After the cooling-off period you may cancel at any time by writing to us. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period for which you have paid. Term life cover has no cash value, so no refund is given for a period that has already started.

## 8. Complaints

8.1 If you are unhappy with our service or a decision, please write to complaints@evergreen-mutual.example.com. Include your policy number and a description of the problem. We will confirm that we received your complaint within 3 working days and send a final response within 30 days.

8.2 If you are not satisfied with our final response, or we have not responded within 30 days, you may refer the matter to the Fictional Insurance Ombudsman Service, a free and independent dispute service created for this demonstration. You must refer the complaint within 6 months of the date of our final response. The Ombudsman Service can make a decision that binds us but not you, and using it does not affect your right to take legal action.
